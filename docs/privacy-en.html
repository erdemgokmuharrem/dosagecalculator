In short

  • The patient details you enter never leave your device. We run no server and do not collect them.
  • The free version shows ads. Depending on the consent you give, limited data is sent to Google for that purpose.
  • Purchases are handled by the store (App Store / Google Play) and RevenueCat; your payment details never reach us.
  • No account is needed; there is no sign-up and no login.

1. Data controller

2. Data that stays on your device

The following is stored only in the app's own storage on your device. It is not sent to us, not uploaded to any server and not backed up by us:

  • The age, weight, height and serum creatinine you enter for a calculation
  • Your calculation history and the drugs you selected
  • Your language and theme preference
  • The fact that you accepted the medical disclaimer
  • A summary of the advertising-consent outcome that contains no identifiers (only status such as "personalised ads on/off")

You can delete all of it by clearing the history in the app or by uninstalling the app.

3. Data sent to third parties

Service Purpose Data processed
Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) Showing ads in the free version and collecting your consent Advertising identifier (only if you allow it), IP address, device and operating-system information, ad impression and click data, fraud-prevention data, your consent choice
RevenueCat and the store (App Store / Google Play) Validating and restoring the Pro subscription An anonymous app user id, purchase and subscription status, country and device information. Your payment details are handled by the store and are not passed to us

The app's own code makes no other network request. The drug database ships inside the app, so search and calculation work offline.

PDF export

When you share a drug monograph as a PDF, the file is produced on your device and goes only to the app you choose. It does not reach us.

4. Advertising consent and tracking permission

Ads appear only in the free version. Personalised ads are shown only if you both allow it in Google's consent form and grant the iOS tracking permission ("App Tracking Transparency"). If either is missing, ads are non-personalised.

  • Where it is required, you can change your consent in the app under Settings → Ad preferences.
  • On iOS you can withdraw the tracking permission in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Refusing either does not limit any calculation feature.

5. Legal basis

Under GDPR (and Turkey's KVKK), ad personalisation and tracking rely on your explicit consent. Purchase validation is necessary to perform the contract, and crash reporting relies on the legitimate interest of keeping the app secure and working.

6. Retention

Data on your device stays there until you delete it. Data sent to third parties is retained according to each service's own policy (see the links below).
